Staff Platform Engineer — Observability & Data Cloud

Coruscant Systems Group is hiring a Staff Platform Engineer to own our observability
stack and our migration to Google Cloud Platform (GCP). You'll report to the Director
of Infrastructure and partner closely with the data platform team.

What you'll do:
- Design and operate our GCP footprint (GKE, Cloud Run, BigQuery) as we migrate off
  our current provider.
- Own GitOps delivery via ArgoCD across every environment — this is a hard requirement,
  not a nice-to-have.
- Build and maintain our Prometheus + Grafana observability stack, including SLO
  dashboards and alerting for every production service.
- Administer our Snowflake data warehouse, including query performance tuning and
  cost governance.
- Mentor engineers on platform reliability practices and lead incident response.

What we're looking for:
- 6+ years in platform/infrastructure engineering roles.
- Deep, hands-on GCP experience — this role owns our GCP migration end to end.
- Production ArgoCD experience — you'll be the org's ArgoCD expert from day one.
- Prometheus and Grafana in production, not just familiarity from a side project.
- Snowflake administration experience.
- Strong Kubernetes fundamentals (any cloud) and comfort with Terraform.
- Excellent written communication — you'll be documenting runbooks other teams rely on.

Location: Remote (US). Compensation: $185,000–$225,000 base + equity.
